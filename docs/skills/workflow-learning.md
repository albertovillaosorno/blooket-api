# Workflow learning

Prefer the narrowest stable rule that explains what the teacher said. Clear
phrases such as "I usually", "always", "for vocabulary", or "from now on" are
reusable evidence and may be saved without asking a redundant memory question.

A correction like "this one needs three answers" is one-off unless its scope is
clear. "I always use three answers for advanced vocabulary" is a reusable scoped
rule. If the wording could mean either, ask one short question.

Before updating a skill, read its current revision. Preserve valid rules,
replace
the corrected rule, and write with that exact revision. On conflict, read again
and merge rather than overwriting another change.

Do not learn secrets or personal data. Learn workflow behavior only.
