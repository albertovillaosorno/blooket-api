# Image-bank research and metadata

Prefer provider-supported search pages, feeds, or APIs. Use bounded requests,
normal caching, and conservative pacing. Do not hammer providers or evade access
controls.

Keep source metadata that is actually present: provider, page, title or caption,
creator when shown, and license or usage label when shown. Missing license data
is `unknown`.

Do not burden the teacher with generic legal lectures. Surface a rights or
attribution issue when it materially affects the requested use, the provider
requires it, or the teacher asks.
